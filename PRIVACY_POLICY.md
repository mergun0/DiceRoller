# Privacy Policy

Dice Roller / Zar At does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal data.

The app works fully offline. It does not request internet access, location, contacts, account access, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, Firebase, or cloud services.

The app stores only local preferences and the latest roll history on the device. Roll history is used only to show recent dice results inside the app and is not sent outside the device.

Users can clear roll history from the app settings or history screen.
