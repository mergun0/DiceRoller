# Dice Roller

## Short Description

An offline dice roller with a clean real 3D dice animation.

## Long Description

Dice Roller is a simple offline dice app for tabletop games and quick decisions. Choose single or double dice, see the result and total, and keep a small local history of recent rolls.

Dice faces are generated inside the app and rendered with OpenGL ES as a real 3D cube animation. The app does not request internet access and does not include ads or analytics.

## Features

- Single and double dice modes
- Real 3D dice rotation
- Programmatic dice themes: Classic, Night, Red, Navy Gold
- Latest 20 rolls stored locally
- Sound, vibration, and shake-to-roll settings
- Light, dark, and system app theme
- Fully offline

## Privacy Summary

Dice Roller does not collect personal data. Roll history and settings stay on the device. There is no internet permission, advertising, analytics, or account sign-in.
